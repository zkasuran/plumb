---
id: PL-HERO
title: The hero says what the visitor can do and offers the action
severity: blocking
applies: [landing]
---
## Rule
The first screen leads with the visitor's job. One heading says what the product does for them in plain words, and one primary button or link starts that job. The heading never describes a test, an audit or an incident.

## Why
Visitors decide in seconds. A heading that names the product's category or reads like a report gives them nothing to do. A heading that names the job and a button that starts it gets them moving.

## Check
- Look at the top of the page without scrolling. Store the main heading text as 'hero'. Verify the stored hero text tells a visitor what they can do or get with the product, in everyday words, and does not describe a test, audit, report or incident.
- Verify a primary call-to-action button or link is visible without scrolling and its label names an action, such as "Start", "Try", "Get", "Install", "Borrow" or "Browse".

## Fix
Rewrite the main heading as the job, for example "Borrow a tool. Lend one." instead of "Tool lending platform". Put one primary button directly under it with a verb label. Move proof, verification and security wording to a supporting section further down.

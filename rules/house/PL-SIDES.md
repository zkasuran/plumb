---
id: PL-SIDES
title: Both sides of a two-sided product get a path and a section
severity: advisory
applies: [landing]
---
## Rule
If the product serves two kinds of people, such as buyers and sellers or borrowers and lenders, each side has its own path card and its own short section. A one-sided product says plainly who it is for.

## Why
A page that only speaks to one side loses the other. Selling or lending should be as visible as buying or borrowing.

## Check
- Scroll through the page. Verify the page either gives each kind of user, for example buyers and sellers, its own labelled card or section, or states plainly in one sentence who the product is for.

## Fix
Add a second path card for the missing side with its own action button. Add a short "how it works in three steps" strip for each side. Show both sides' commands if the product has a CLI.

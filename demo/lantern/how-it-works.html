Good questions

The practical things.

A little clarity makes sharing easier.

Is this a real neighbourhood collection?

This is a sample Lantern neighbourhood. Listings and borrowing plans you add stay in this browser. No request is sent to an owner.

Does a borrowing plan reserve a tool?

No. A plan is a reminder on your device. Arrange pickup and return directly with the owner before borrowing.

What should I check before borrowing?

Check the condition, accessories and instructions with the owner. Use suitable protective equipment and only borrow tools you're confident using.

What does it cost?

The sample tools are free to borrow. Agree on any arrangements with the owner before starting your project.
